Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, place an order, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your culinary orders
  • Communicate with you about your order status
  • Send you exclusive Durban deals (if opted in)
  • Improve our premium services

3. Data Security & Protection

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is stored in encrypted databases and is only accessible by authorized personnel with strict access controls.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You can manage your account details through your Client Hub or contact us at privacy@lollys.co.za for assistance.
