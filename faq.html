Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Durban's most authentic food experience.

Is your food 100% Halal?

Yes, Lolly's Food Joint is fully Halal certified. We source our ingredients from strictly Halal-compliant suppliers and maintain the highest standards of preparation.

How long does preparation take?

Most orders are ready for collection within 15-20 minutes. During peak Durban lunch hours, it may take up to 30 minutes. You'll receive a notification when your meal is ready.

Do you offer delivery?

We currently focus on collection orders via our website to ensure maximum freshness. However, you can find us on major third-party delivery apps in the Durban area.

Can I pay with my Lolly's Wallet?

Absolutely! You can top up your wallet via your Client Hub and use the balance for instant, secure payments at checkout.
